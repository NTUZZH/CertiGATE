## I. Second price against the site's own schedule

A variant of the second price that needs no lower bound. It replaces the bound of the untouched known board by the weighted tardiness of that board as the site's dispatching rule schedules it: s2_site = (obj_orig_K - WT_anchor_K) / max(WT_anchor_K, 1 bh), and a field edit is referred when s2_site > tau. obj_orig_K is the executed schedule's weighted tardiness on the known board scored on the original priorities and time windows (column obj_orig). WT_anchor_K is column ref1 of analysis/N11_rows_direct.csv and analysis/N11_rows_e2e.csv (the reference of D-REL1); under the three other rules it is column anchor_K of analysis/N14_rows.csv. The bound-referenced second price of blocks 0, D1 and D2 is s2 = (obj_orig_K - LB_anchor_K) / max(LB_anchor_K, 1 bh). Costly, low-cost, benign, applied and referred are defined as in blocks C and D. First prices are read at their published thresholds, and either second price is applied to field edits only. WT_anchor_K is at least LB_anchor_K, so s2_site > tau implies s2 > tau; the variant refers a subset of the field edits that s2 refers (asserted on every row).
