# N27b: the gate on a known board from which planned and corrective orders are withheld (l1-n27b-withheld-all-1)

Generated by `code/scripts/n27b_withheld_all.py` on cores 8 and 9 (one thread per process): deterministic replays of the direct benchmark's known-board pricing (n11_decision_time.gen_price, no solver, no time budget), no model call, no GPU. Post hoc analysis. Frozen designs: `results/n27_withheld_orders/design.md` (sha256 db71daa42aa711e888c55a1434ae8cfc2d221e232b2ed7295bcca5ece0734625) and `results/n27_withheld_orders/design_n27b_2026-11-11.md` (sha256 18b8dc72ef98f4d42045bf32af6ebfa606398e896ada81eb0348a1d80baa5c96), addendum `results/n27_withheld_orders/addendum_n27b.md`.

This variant departs from the benchmark's premise that the maintenance calendar is known at the decision instant. It stresses the information the gate is given and makes no claim about a site.

At level f the gate prices each of the 1,356 priced proposals on the known board K at t_d minus floor(f |H2| + 0.5) of the candidate orders H2 (not started, queued or on the calendar, planned or corrective, named neither by the instruction nor by the proposal, not frozen), drawn at random (five draws at f = 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, one at f = 1, which withholds H2). The labels are the stored ones. Refused = stopped by the schema or feasibility check on the thinned board, or ended in an instrument fault. Mean and range are over draws. Low-cost shares carry the n2 cluster bootstrap interval on draw 0.
