Generated by `code/scripts/n26_shift_keeps_due.py` on cores 8 and 9 (one thread per process): deterministic dispatcher replays (n11_decision_time.gen_price, no solver, no time budget), no model call, no GPU. Post hoc sensitivity analysis. Frozen design: `results/n26_shift_keeps_due/design.md` (sha256 db8a8e6b36c76888563be05350e35c3b18f0cb73969ce9c52aa3eedec4c58bf9), addendum `results/n26_shift_keeps_due/addendum.md`.

Convention B is the benchmark: `reassign_window` moves the release and the due date (the SLA clock restarts). Convention D moves the release only and keeps the due date. Everything else is unchanged. K = known board at t_d, FI = full-information board at t_d, realised = the whole-window run from t_d. Certificate at tau = 0.20 on the board's own bound (LB2 on the fields the guard is given). Second price on field edits: (objective on the original values minus the no-proposal bound) over that bound, above 0.20. bh = business hours.
