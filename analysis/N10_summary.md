# N10. Field-changing proposals: certified gap on the adjusted fields against damage on the original fields

Generated by `code/scripts/n10_field_change.py` (l1-n10-field-change-1). 174682 assertions passed. Deterministic: no dispatch, no time budget, no model call, no timestamp.

## Population and definitions

- Guard stress test in constrained mode, all ten (arm, thinking) cells, every repeat, pooled unweighted over rows as Table 10 pools them: 40,000 rows, of which 29,980 pass the schema and feasibility gates and reach the quality gate.
- Split by the logged operation list (`n2_pricing.op_split`, equal to column 15 of `analysis/N2_rows_e2e.csv` on every row): empty, sequencing-only, field-changing (any `set_priority` or `reassign_window`).
- Class: benign twins, V3 (cost-bearing), V4 (mistranslation), other (V1, V2, V5, V6).
- Certified gap: the logged G_CERT `certificate_gap`, computed on the adjusted fields. Applied when the gap is at most tau = 0.20.
- Damage: executed weighted tardiness on the original fields minus the no-AI anchor on the original fields, in business hours (bh), built exactly as `e1_delta_guard_replay.py` builds `mean_excess_bh`.
- Original-field reading: (executed weighted tardiness on the original fields minus LB2 of the unadjusted instance) / max(LB2, 1). LB2 of the unadjusted instance is the anchor record's `certificate.lb_bh`; it equals the logged lower bound of every empty and sequencing-only proposal on the same instance (asserted).
- Pooled scopes: `all` (ten cells) and `capability` (the eight cells without DeepSeek's no-schema mode).

## Assertions

- The per-row damage reproduces `analysis/T5_ladder.csv` (mean excess against the anchor, mean executed objective, three pass-through readings; G-FEAS and G-CERT; all ten cells) and the G-CERT headline `mean_excess_bh` of `analysis/DG17_e2e_delta.csv` for all ten cells.
- The log digests equal the digests DG17 recorded.
- The 29,980 quality-reaching rows join 1:1 to the constrained end-to-end rows of `analysis/N2_rows_e2e.csv`, with equal split, certified gap, objective, operation count and row id.
- Applied iff the logged gap is at most tau, on every row; zero-operation rows execute the anchor exactly; empty and sequencing-only rows have equal original- and adjusted-field objectives and an original-field reading equal to the certified gap.
- Every unguarded-replay row joins to a constrained row stopped before the quality gate.
