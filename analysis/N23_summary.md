# N23: urgent orders made late by a proposal, and intervals for the stress-test table (l1-n23-urgent-1)

Generated by `code/scripts/n23_urgent_orders.py` on cores 12-13 (one thread per process): deterministic dispatcher replays (n11_decision_time.gen_price, no solver, no time budget) and recounts of the logged results; no model call, no GPU. Reused: n20.build's records (n16/n18/n13 loaded from source with the core allowance set to 12-13 in memory; files untouched), n13.costly_of, the published flags, n20's second price, n2.Boot (seed 20261026, 20000 replicates, 60 instance clusters), n19's Table 6 records and measures. Macros: analysis/N23_macros.tex (prefix \nTwentyThree), copied to manuscript/macros_n23.tex. Rows: analysis/N23_rows.csv.

Definitions. Urgent order = an order of priority class 1 or 2 on the original fields (Section sec:prob-env, Table tab:env-provenance: class 1 has the 24-hour response target, SLA 8 bh, tardiness weight 8; class 2 the 72-hour target, SLA 24 bh, weight 4; classes 3 and 4 have 14 and 30 days, SLA 80 and 171.4 bh, weights 2 and 1; planned maintenance takes class 4). Newly late = finishes on time without the proposal and late with it, on the original due date (C - d > 1e-9 bh; schedule times carry four decimals). Realised = the full-window run from t_d with the proposal against the run without it (the schedule the realised damage is measured against; its weighted tardiness equals the no-AI anchor). Applied = not referred by the design on the stated reading. Low-cost / costly = the whole-board label at delta. bh = business hours (eight per weekday).

## 0. Published headline and the replay

The published headline of the direct benchmark at delta = tau = 0.20 is asserted first by n16.section0, n18.section0 and n20.section0 on n20.build's records (1,356 priced proposals, 186 costly, 1,170 low-cost; G-CERT applies 18 costly and refers 15 low-cost; with the second price 0 and 22), and the six designs' published flags are tied to N16/N15: G-CERT 18 / 15, G-CERT with the second price 0 / 22, G-CERT-M 18 / 14, D-REL1 18 / 80, D-REL2 18 / 33, D-ABS 17 / 81 (costly applied / low-cost referred at 0.20).

Replay: 1356 proposals on 60 instances, 459 decision-instant sites (instance x frozen set x t_d); per proposal three boards with the proposal (realised full window, known board, full-information board) and per site the same three without it, all through n11_decision_time.gen_price (ATC, seed 0, the site's dispatcher) with N11's frozen-set convention 'fix'. Wall time 392 s on cores [12, 13] (2 workers, one thread each).

The run without the proposal is the schedule the realised damage is measured against: at every site its start and end time of every order equal those of the baseline trajectory, whose schedule digest equals the no-AI anchor's schedule_digest in analysis/ladder/rule_anchor.json (asserted per site; the digest of the run itself can differ only by the label of an identical technician of the same trade, the case n8_causal.pin_schedule describes), and its weighted tardiness equals the anchor's (below).
