# N12: gate sensitivity to the environment's derived settings

Status: **COMPLETE** (5 of 5 environments analysed). Generated by `code/scripts/n12_env_sensitivity.py report` (l1-n12-env-sensitivity-1). Every number is in `analysis/N12_rows.csv` and the headline cells are macros `\nTwelve<Setting><Quantity>` in `analysis/N12_macros.tex`.

## What was computed

Five environments: the calibrated one and four perturbed ones (windows: due = release + f SLA[p] on every order, f = 0.5 and 1.5; crews: max(1, round(n f)) technicians per trade, f = 0.75 and 1.25). Each environment's 60 instances are derived in memory from the manifest-verified instances_v10 snapshot (hashes in `results/n12_env/manifest_n12.json`); the scaled SLA clock is bound in every process, so set_priority and reassign_window restart it. On each environment the direct benchmark (canonical proposal of every suite item; labels and thresholds frozen at tau = 0.20, D-REL1 0.02, D-REL2 0.02, D-ABS 0.8119) is read three ways: (1) the full-window reading at t = 0 against new full-window oracle brackets (oracle_v1's recipe, 60 s per trade, 4 workers); (2) the decision-time reading on K at t_d = t_known against the decision-time oracle on K and the realised consequence on the original fields; (3) the forecast reading F2 at S = 20 (Stage 3's smoke decision, top-up to 50 near a threshold).

The calibrated column is computed by the same code path with f = 1 and reproduces the published reading (assertion E6): N11's census rows and boards, N2's direct rows and headline cells, oracle_v1's brackets, N11's decision-time brackets and Stage 3's F2 prices, exactly.
