### The sign-flip permutation test of Section 4.6.2

Section 4.6.2 (the manuscript text, sec:setup-stats): "A paired comparison uses a sign-flip permutation test that flips all paired differences from one instance together", and the primary comparison is the certificate on the known board against the full-information gate at delta = tau = 0.20; app_methods_detail.tex: "The sign-flip permutation test is enumerated exactly." Implementation reused: code/scripts/e3_intervals.py (cluster_index_arrays, cluster_sums, signflip_exact_p; the DG18 cluster test). Paired difference per proposal = indicator on the known board minus indicator with full information; statistic = the sum over instances (= count on K minus count with FI); two-sided p over all 2^k sign vectors of the k instances whose differences do not cancel. The direct benchmark has one proposal per instruction and no repeat, so 'repeat 0' is the whole population.
