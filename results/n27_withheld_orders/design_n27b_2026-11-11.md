# N27b design addendum (2026-11-11, frozen): planned and corrective orders may be withheld

Version l1-n27b-withheld-all-1. This file extends the frozen N27 design (design.md, sha256
db71daa42aa711e888c55a1434ae8cfc2d221e232b2ed7295bcca5ece0734625, which stays byte-unchanged).
It is written and its sha256 recorded (design_n27b_2026-11-11.sha256) before any pricing of
this variant. Later choices go to addendum_n27b.md in this folder; this file is never edited.

## Status

Post hoc. N27 found that the corrective-only candidate set is too small to thin the gate's
board (H empty for 648 of 1,356 priced proposals, at most 63 orders), because the known board
is a median 95% planned maintenance. This variant also lets planned orders be withheld. It
departs from the benchmark's premise that the maintenance calendar is known at the decision
instant. It is a stress on the information the gate is given, not a claim about any site.

## What changes

Only the candidate rule. H2 = every order on the known board K at t_d that (i) has not
started at t_d (the queue, planned or corrective, and the planned orders on the calendar
released after t_d; running and completed orders are not candidates), (ii) is named neither
by the instruction (`referenced.order_ids`) nor by any operation of the proposal
(n8_causal.named_orders: the order ids of every operation and the members of every batch group
it names), and (iii) is not in the standing frozen set (n11_decision_time.kf_fix). Planned and
corrective orders are treated alike.

Draws: n_w = floor(f x |H2| + 0.5) orders of H2, without replacement, with
random.Random(seed) over the sorted ids of H2, seed = the first 16 hex digits of
sha256("all|<item id>|<f with two decimals>|<draw index>") read as an integer (the prefix
"all|" keeps these draws apart from N27's).

## What stays as in design.md

The 1,356 priced reference proposals, their t_d and stored labels at delta 0.20 and 0.10; the
levels f in {0, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00}, five draws (0 to 4) below 1 and one at 1, f = 0 the
self-check; the board construction, frozen set, pin schedule, running orders, technicians,
ATC, seed 0, the guard's stages, the bound and the floor; the pricing call
(n11_decision_time.gen_price with ref2 and the running-work rule, and the no-proposal run on
the same board); every design at its own threshold (G-CERT 0.20, G-CERT-M 0.20, D-REL1 0.02,
D-REL2 0.02, D-ABS 0.8119 weighted bh) and the certificate with the second price on field
edits; refused = stopped by stage 1 or 2 or an instrument fault; one pricing per distinct
board; the checks of section 5 (frozen files, adapter hashes, frozen counts, N18's later
share, the f = 0 tie for all 1,356 proposals); the n2 cluster bootstrap on draw 0.

## Additional checks

- The micro-instance probe with H2: queued and calendar planned orders and unnamed queued
  corrective orders are candidates; running, named and frozen orders are not; withholding
  removes exactly the drawn orders.
- f = 0 rows equal N27's f = 0 rows (analysis/N27_rows.csv) in every price, the second price
  and every disposition, for all 1,356 proposals.

## Reported (delta = 0.20 first, then 0.10)

Items 1 to 6 of design.md section 6, with these changes:
- Item 5 gives, per level, for the costly proposals and for all priced proposals, the median,
  quartiles and maximum of both the share of the window's orders not seen,
  (n_unknown + n_w) / n_window, and the share of the known board withheld, n_w / |K|; plus
  the distribution of |H2| / |K|.
- Item 6 lists every costly proposal whose disposition under the certificate or the
  certificate with the second price differs from f = 0 in some draw of some level (either
  direction, or refused): class, family, edit type, the certificate price and the second price
  at each level and draw with the orders withheld, and the realised-cost interval.
- If any disposition of a costly proposal changes, for each delta and each design: the lowest
  level at which a change occurs in some draw, and the smallest share of the window not seen
  (and of K withheld) among the proposal-draws with a change.
- Descriptive, as in N27: the composition of K (5b) and the movement of the certificate price
  (6b).
