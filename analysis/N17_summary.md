## 1. Definitions

Suites: `build_suite.py --seed S --out code/suite/v1.1_heldout_S` for S in 20261110, 20261111, 20261112, the published SuiteConfig otherwise (suite_version v0.2, quotas, screens, templates, strata, the 60 pool windows and the instance stride). The direct benchmark of the paper is built on suite v0.2 alone (build_suite.py); the v1.1 extensions (build_suite_ext.py, build_suite_ext_neg.py) add the acceptance-clause items of the N3 run and are not part of the direct benchmark, so they are not redrawn.

Held-out set: the items of a fresh suite whose (instance, instruction text) and whose (instance, gold operations) do not occur in the published suite. Empty gold-operation lists are not used as a key: 515 published items (V1 160, V5 200, V6 155) carry the empty list, so the key would mark every such fresh item a duplicate without any shared content.

Replay: per instance, n11_decision_time.census_instance (issuability under the closed rule, t_d = t_known, the known board K with the fixed frozen set, the gate's price on K with the analytic lower bound, D-REL2's reference, the realised full-window run from t_d) and n11_forecast.forecast_instance with the single reading FI. Prices: n11_decision_time.build_rows (n2.prices). Realised cost: n11_forecast.attach_orig_truth = (realised weighted tardiness on the original values minus the published no-AI anchor) / max(full-window optimum of the untouched window, 1 bh), bracketed by the oracle_v1 bracket of the untouched window (exists for all 60). Costly: lower end above delta; low-cost: upper end at most delta; otherwise undecided and left out of both counts (n13.costly_of).

Gates (the frozen set): the certificate G-CERT on K at tau = 0.20; with FI: the same gate on the board with every order of the window known; with the second price: n16.sp_flag, i.e. also refer a field edit when g_orig = (obj_orig_K - LB_anchor_K) / max(LB_anchor_K, 1 bh) > 0.20; the site-schedule variant: the same with s2_site = (obj_orig_K - WT_anchor_K) / max(WT_anchor_K, 1 bh); D-REL2 at its published 0.02 and at 0.20, each with and without the second price. Applied = a costly proposal the gate does not refer. Intervals: n2.Boot cluster bootstrap over the 60 instances (seed 20261026, 20000 replicates); pooled intervals resample instances with all three seeds' items of an instance together.
